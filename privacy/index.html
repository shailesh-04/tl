Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

TL is a collection of static web pages. It has no backend, no database, and no server that receives your input. This policy explains exactly what that means in practice.

What we collect

Nothing. TL does not ask for an email address, does not use an account system, and does not run analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts of any kind.

What happens to your files and text

Every tool on this site processes your input locally, in your own browser, using JavaScript and — for the video converter — FFmpeg compiled to WebAssembly. Spreadsheets, CSV files, JSON payloads, images, and video files are read from disk by your browser, transformed in memory, and handed back to you as a download or a clipboard copy. They are never transmitted to any server and nothing is retained once you close the tab.

Local storage

Two features store data on your own device and nowhere else:

  • The JSON Converter & Data Filter can save filter configurations to your browser’s local storage so they are available next time.
  • Some pages remember your theme preference the same way.

This data stays in your browser, is readable only by this site on your device, and is removed when you clear your browser data.

Third-party services

Pages load styling and typography from public CDNs — Tailwind CSS from cdn.tailwindcss.com, fonts from Google Fonts, and a few libraries from jsDelivr and cdnjs. As with any resource request, those providers can see your IP address and user agent. They receive none of your file contents or tool input. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own standard server logs.

Cookies

TL sets no cookies.

Children

The site collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions or corrections are welcome as an issue on the GitHub repository.
